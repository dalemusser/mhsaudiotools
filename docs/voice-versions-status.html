Dialogue voices & ElevenLabs v3 support

Good news: every voice we use renders on v3, accepts emotion tags, and returns word timings — verified by live test. The one wrinkle is the five professional-clone voices: v3 renders them without their professional fine-tuning, so they may sound less like the intended voice than they do on v2. That's a listen-and-decide call per voice.

Verified by live synthesis against the project account on 21 July 2026 — every voice returned audio on eleven_v3, and v3's /with-timestamps returned a populated alignment. The pro-clone caveat comes from the model flag serves_pro_voices = false (v3 doesn't apply the professional fine-tuning), so judge those by ear.

What this means for emotion

The plan is to support both v2 and v3, choosing per voice and applying emotion tags only on the v3 ones.

Emotion is on the table for everyone

v3 audio tags — [excited], [sighs], [whispers] — work on every voice we use, and v3 still returns word timings for captions. Nothing is blocked.

The pro-clone tradeoff

Five voices are professional clones. On v3 they lose their professional fine-tuning, so they may drift from today's sound. For each, decide: v2 (best likeness, flat delivery) or v3 (emotion, base render).

  • Aryn (Haseeb)
  • Mission Control (Dorian Chase)
  • ABIE (Finn)
  • Player 1 (Brayden), Player 2 (Miranda)

How to judge it

Use the app's ▶ preview to hear a pro-clone voice on v3 with a tag, next to its v2 version, and pick. The generated voices (everything else) are an easy yes to v3.

Loose end

Automated System (Thomas) isn't in the account's voice library — its ID resolves for playback, but confirm the voice and re-save it before relying on it.
